A Law Firm SEO Audit Built Around Qualified Consultations

A law firm SEO audit should explain what prospective clients can find, what they miss, and what happens after they arrive. Law Firm Audit reviews your search presence alongside your website, tracking, and intake path so the findings lead to a practical plan.

In brief: A law firm SEO audit checks whether search engines can find the right pages, whether those pages answer client questions, and whether visits lead to consultations. The result is a prioritized list of improvements.

What we examine

We start with the pages you already publish, including your homepage, practice-area pages, location pages, and contact path. We check whether each page serves a clear client question, whether its title and description match that intent, and whether important pages can be discovered and indexed. We review internal links and repeated topics that may split attention between pages.

Local search matters when your firm serves a defined area. We examine the consistency of location and service information on the site and identify opportunities to make practice and geography clear. We separate observed evidence from recommendations: a search result, a crawl finding, and an idea for a new page are different things.

Connect search traffic to intake

Visibility alone does not show whether a page helps your firm. We look at calls to action, contact forms, mobile usability, and the follow-up path. Where your analytics and Search Console data are available, we compare page visibility with visits and meaningful actions. Missing tracking is called out rather than treated as zero performance.

What the audit delivers

You receive a prioritized list of page, local SEO, measurement, and intake improvements. Each recommendation identifies the page involved, the evidence behind it, and the next action. We distinguish changes you can make to an existing page from topics that deserve a dedicated page. A rank check provides a dated baseline; later checks can show whether published changes are followed by better visibility. Search results vary, so no position is guaranteed.

Tell us your website, main practice areas, and the markets you serve. We will use that context to focus the review on relevant searches and the consultation path that matters to your team.
